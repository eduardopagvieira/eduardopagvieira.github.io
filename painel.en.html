Consumer, BNDES and the Rouanet Law

I put the three projects here for anyone who wants to play with the numbers. Each tab has filters and a question that guided the analysis.

I wrote this dashboard’s code with help from AI. The numbers come from my analysis scripts, open on GitHub, and I checked each one against the results of every project.

Who solves the customer’s problem, and who only answers?

2.3 million complaints filed on Consumidor.gov.br in 2025, against 1,741 companies. Answering is almost universal. Solving, as rated by the consumers themselves, is far rarer.

Where I’d use this

  • Compare service with competitors in the same sector.
  • Separate answering from solving, and track both.
  • Read official metrics carefully, since the platform’s index counts every unrated complaint as solved.

Where does BNDES credit go, and how has it changed since 2002?

2.4 million financing operations, inflation-adjusted. The bank multiplied credit between 2009 and 2014 and then fell back to a much lower level.

Where I’d use this

  • Map sectors, firm sizes and regions where long-term credit is growing.
  • Compare periods on the same basis, already IPCA-adjusted.
  • Build time series ready for market intelligence reports.

Where does Rouanet Law incentivized sponsorship go?

Cultural projects approved from 2019 to 2025. Almost all the money raised comes from companies that deduct 100% of the sponsorship from income tax, and fundraising concentrates in a few applicants and in the Southeast.

Where I’d use this

  • Plan sponsorship and ESG actions knowing where the money is already concentrated.
  • Find regions and cultural areas with little fundraising and more visibility per real invested.
  • See the same content in the Power BI dashboard on GitHub.
